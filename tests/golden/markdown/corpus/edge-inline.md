**strong with *nested em* inside** plus *em with **nested strong** inside*.

line` code.

Links: [inline](https://example.com), [same](same), [titled](https://x.org "Title"),
[**bold link**](https://b.org), [ref link][ref], [collapsed][], [shortcut].

Images: ![alt text](img.png), ![*styled* alt](a.png), ![](empty.png).

Entities: &amp; &lt;tag&gt; &copy; &#35; &#x41; and escapes: \* \_ \` \\ \[.

Inline HTML: <span class="x">text</span> and <br/> and <!-- comment -->.
